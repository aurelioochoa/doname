# Assets de dóname

Modo: herramienta integrada imagegen. Los originales están en `design/originals/`; las versiones WebP optimizadas están en `dist/assets/`. Las imágenes ilustran ejemplos y no corresponden a donaciones ni organizaciones reales.

## Portada

Archivo: `dist/assets/community.webp`.

Prompt:
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: photographic website hero for dóname, an Ecuadorian community giving platform. Create an authentic, warm editorial photograph of two ordinary Ecuadorian women, one in her late twenties and one about sixty, smiling naturally as they hand over a medium cardboard box containing folded clothes and a small green plant, in a sunlit community courtyard in Quito, soft stucco architecture and lush green leaves softly blurred in the background. The photograph should feel candid, dignified and optimistic, no poverty stereotypes, no staged commercial handshake. Modern documentary color grading, natural warm daylight, muted green clothing, real skin texture. Landscape 3:2 framing, waist up, people and box centered with some breathing room around all edges. No text, no logos, no watermark. Website will crop this photograph inside a rounded arch.

## Silla de ruedas

Archivo: `dist/assets/wheelchair.webp`.

Prompt:
Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: listing photo for an Ecuadorian social donation platform. A single simple used but clean black manual folding wheelchair, with large silver-spoked rear wheels, small front caster wheels, black padded seat and back, footrests. Parked on pale stone in a welcoming sunlit Quito courtyard with soft green plants and cream stucco behind. 3/4 angle view, wheelchair fully visible, subject centered, landscape 4:3 photo. Authentic dignified everyday product photography, not a hospital, warm natural light, realistic materials, muted greens, no people, no text, no logo, no watermark.

## Acompañamiento a personas mayores

Archivo: `dist/assets/seniors.webp`.

Prompt:
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: thumbnail photograph for a social volunteering website in Ecuador. Primary request: a warm, dignified candid editorial photograph of an older Ecuadorian woman about 75 and a young adult Ecuadorian woman volunteer about 25 enjoying a conversation and reading a book together at a wooden table in a sunlit community garden. Genuine everyday clothing in muted green and soft cream, natural smiles, realistic skin and hands, welcoming and optimistic atmosphere, blurred plants, soft natural daylight. Landscape 4:3 composition, both people visible at waist up, subjects centered. No hospital, no medical uniforms, no poverty stereotypes, no text, no logos, no watermark.
